HTML Entities

Special characters and reserved symbols in HTML:

Less than: < (Code: &lt;)

Greater than: > (Code: &gt;)

Ampersand: & (Code: &amp;)

Double Quote: " (Code: &quot;)

Single Quote: ' (Code: &apos;)

Copyright: © (Code: &copy;)

Registered: ® (Code: &reg;)

Indian Rupee: ₹ (Code: &#8377;)

Euro: € (Code: &euro;)

Non-breaking Space: First Second (Code: &nbsp;)
